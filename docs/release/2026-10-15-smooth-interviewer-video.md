Original media, voices, interview state, captions and controls are retained. Both clips preload; the inactive clip pauses after the handoff to limit ongoing decoding work.
* **Root-Cause Architectural Failure:** CallView keyed a single video by speaking/listening state. Each transition discarded the playing element and created a new decoder surface, exposing the light background before the next file presented a frame.
* **Production-Grade Implementation:** Two stable, muted video elements share the existing stage geometry. The listening surface remains opaque underneath the speaking layer, so a crossfade does not uncover the background. Presentation waits for `requestVideoFrameCallback`, with a current-data/two-animation-frame fallback. A matching portrait protects initial loading. Cancelled effects invalidate stale callbacks, cancel scheduled frames and pause captured media elements on cleanup. A delayed or unavailable incoming clip leaves the previous image in place. Pause, hidden-tab handling and reduced motion remain supported. New styling uses Tailwind utilities.
