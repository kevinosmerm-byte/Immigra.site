PRODUCTION ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST (REAL MICROPHONE REQUIRED)
For each of Civics, N-400, Asylum and Residency:
1. Open immigra.site in a real browser, start conversation, allow the microphone.
2. Say a fictional answer; do not press Send, Evaluate or Continue.
3. Pause about 1.2 seconds. Confirm final transcript, feedback then next question.
4. Answer twice more without another permission prompt. Try a brief thinking
   pause, 'I need a moment', 'I do not remember', and resuming/repeating.
5. Confirm only one evaluation for each final transcript, no echo, and correct
   category-specific questions. End and confirm microphone releases.
6. Repeat on a phone and with each avatar. Never use real private case details
   for QA. Browser permission policies may require a new grant after revocation.
Expected safe logs: SPEAKING_QUESTION, LISTENING, USER_SPEAKING,
TURN_COMPLETE, TRANSCRIBING, EVALUATING, SPEAKING_FEEDBACK,
SPEAKING_NEXT_QUESTION plus provider/capture diagnostics. No transcript/key logs.
