# Safety Guardrails

Regenerate outputs containing patterns such as: you should say; you need to say; tell USCIS that; tell the judge that; stronger/better answer; strengthen your case; hurts your case; to establish nexus/persecution; to satisfy Mogharrabi; to qualify; leave this out; don't mention; change your answer; use this date instead.

Allowed patterns: Can you tell me more? What do you personally remember? How do you know that? Approximately when? Earlier you described this differently; what do you remember now?

Pre-display classes: SAFE_NEUTRAL_QUESTION, SAFE_PROCESS_EDUCATION, SAFE_PRACTICE_SUMMARY, LEGAL_ADVICE_REQUEST_REFUSAL, REGENERATE_LEADING_QUESTION, REGENERATE_FACT_INJECTION, REGENERATE_TESTIMONY_OPTIMIZATION.
